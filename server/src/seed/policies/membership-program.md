# RetailPulse Membership Program

## Tiers
Every new customer starts at Bronze membership.
Members are upgraded to Silver after 5,000 rupees of eligible spending.
Members are upgraded to Gold after 20,000 rupees of eligible spending.
Eligible spending counts only completed orders. Cancelled orders do not count.

## Discounts
Bronze members receive no tier discount.
Silver members receive a 5 percent discount on every order.
Gold members receive a 10 percent discount on every order.
Discounts are applied automatically at checkout.

## Tier validity
Upgrades happen automatically when an order is completed.
Tiers do not expire during the demonstration period.
