# Gold Member Exclusive Benefits

Gold members get priority packing, so their orders are packed before other orders.
Gold members have access to a dedicated support line with a response time of under 2 hours.

Gold members receive an additional 6 months of warranty on electronics purchased at RetailPulse.

## Price protection
If the price of a product drops within 7 days of purchase, Gold members can claim the difference as store credit.

## Early access
Gold members get access to seasonal sales 24 hours before other members.
