# Return and Refund Policy

## Return window
Bronze members can return eligible items within 7 days of delivery or pickup.
Silver members can return eligible items within 15 days of delivery or pickup.
Gold members can return eligible items within 30 days of delivery or pickup.

## Eligible items
Items must be unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by the order number.
Groceries, personal care products that have been opened, and innerwear cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.
Electronics can be returned only if the seal is unbroken, unless the item is defective.

## Defective items
Defective items can be returned or replaced within 30 days for all members, regardless of tier.
The store will inspect the item before approving a replacement or refund.

## Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the return is approved.
Takeaway orders can be returned at the same store where they were picked up.
Delivery orders can be returned at any RetailPulse store or by scheduling a pickup.
