# Delivery and Takeaway Policy

## Delivery charges
Bronze members get free delivery on orders of 999 rupees or more. Orders below 999 rupees have a delivery fee of 49 rupees.
Silver members get free delivery on orders of 499 rupees or more.
Gold members get free delivery on all orders.

## Delivery time
Orders placed before 6 PM are delivered the same day within city limits.
Orders placed after 6 PM are delivered the next day.
Delivery is available only within the city of the selected store.

## Takeaway
Takeaway orders are usually ready for pickup within 2 hours.
Customers must show the order number at the store counter.
Unclaimed takeaway orders are cancelled after 3 days and refunded.

## Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are packed.
Once an order is out for delivery or ready for pickup, it cannot be cancelled and must follow the return policy.
